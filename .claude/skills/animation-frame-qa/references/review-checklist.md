# Final render checklist

Adapted from Paldom/storytelling-animation's animation-frame-qa/references/review-checklist.md at 2c6dd3408ac13d3695cf3246da16b1c671a1ed30 (MIT); see ../LICENSE.

1. **Opening:** the first frame names/shows the topic at phone size; the first action is clear, with no blank or black entrance.
2. **Phone readability:** read every important word without zooming. Look for clipped German words, subtitles lost against the background and labels overlapping the actor.
3. **Composition:** inspect the actual subtitles and UI area. Key visuals should fit the repo guide; subtitles belong in their own band and backgrounds may extend beyond it.
4. **Meaning and continuity:** compare the frame with the DE line/source and native per-shot evidence. Identity, room layout, quantity, grip and open/closed states remain consistent.
5. **Movement:** watch the relevant segment at normal speed. Check contact before displacement, feet/body support, a visible result and readable cuts. A contact sheet cannot prove every intervening frame.
6. **Pacing:** look for repeated tableaux, idle zooms or empty holds. Keep a meaningful reaction/reading pause; do not invent motion solely to satisfy a metric. An ending does not need a CTA or forced loop.
7. **Luminance candidates:** inspect reported times. An intended cut or a real light switch may explain a jump; missing assets/fonts can also cause it. Mean luminance alone does not decide the cause or establish flash safety.
8. **Delivery facts:** compare actual duration, size, frame rate and audible content with the job. For these scripts the final film must exceed 60 seconds; do not infer duration from the word-count estimate.
9. **Sound:** listen for clear narration, clipping, intrusive music, unmotivated effects and mis-timed real-world sounds.
10. **Evidence:** a generated PNG/report is evidence to inspect, not a visual pass. Only claim observations actually made; route a defect through the existing review/QA-FIX process.

Fix text/composition defects with vector-scene-layout; fix contact, jerks and timing with smooth-motion-choreography.
Keep native review records authoritative. Reuse unchanged evidence under the current source-signature rules and three-round budget.
