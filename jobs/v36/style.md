# v36 – Der Hauptmann von Köpenick · Style bible

**Mood:** a true caper told like an illustrated archive picture book: dry humour, documentary dignity, never slapstick-cheap. Kênh 3 archive look ("hồ sơ lưu trữ") mixed with kiểu I — the red-brick Rathaus Köpenick is the second main character (opening, guards, statue, ending).

**Palette:** aged paper `#EADBC0` (base), paper shadow `#D8C5A3`, ink brown-black `#3B2F2A` (all outlines), stamp red `#B23A2E` (stamps, numbers, accents), olive `#6B7B4B` / `#8A9A63` (trees, map, desk pad), brown shadow `#8A6E52`; Prussian blue `#24324F` (Voigt's captain coat, a darker blue than the soldiers' `#33507F`), brick `#A8452F` with stone trim `#E2CFAA`, gold `#D9A441` (buttons, spike, coins), bronze `#8C6235` via colour filter for the statue.

**Line & shape:** flat vector, rounded, ink outline 4–7 px `#3B2F2A`, no gradients on characters; paper-grain speckle pattern and a light film flicker over every frame.

**Characters (identical in every shot):**
- Wilhelm Voigt — tall, thin, hollow cheeks, big drooping grey walrus moustache, grey side hair; captain's uniform in dark Prussian blue that is slightly too big (long sleeves, long coat), red collar & cuffs, gold buttons, silver epaulettes and sash, black Pickelhaube with gold spike and eagle plate, sword on the left. Before the coup: patched grey jacket + flat cap (or leather apron as a shoemaker). After the coup: brown coat + black bowler.
- Soldiers — younger round faces, lighter blue tunics, black belts, rifles with bayonets, Pickelhaube.
- Mayor (bald, white whiskers, black frock coat, gold chain of office), city secretary (thin, round glasses, grey suit, quill), cashier (green visor, black sleeve guards, brown vest), police (black-blue, silver spike), cellmate (cap, stubble, patched coat), judge (black robe and beret), Kaiser (grey-blue uniform, upturned moustache). All drawn freely, not from photos or film actors.

**Camera:** a new composition every 1.5–3 s, hard cuts on new lines; wide set → medium → close-up props (calendar, file folder, receipt, clock). Over-the-shoulder for arms that reach into a close-up.

**Archive devices:** red rubber stamps that slam with ink spray (`KÖPENICK · 1906`, `16.10.1906`, `4 JAHRE`), file folder + polaroid, tear-off calendar, newspapers sliding in, typed `QUITTUNG`, `3557 MARK` counter.

**Subtitles:** `default` (dark box) — backgrounds are light paper/daylight.

**Avoid:** real photos or the film's Voigt, invented names/dates, Nazi-era imagery, long sentences on screen.
