**Characters (identical in every shot):**
- Wilhelm Voigt — tall, thin, hollow cheeks, big drooping grey walrus moustache, grey side hair; captain's uniform in dark Prussian blue that is slightly too big (long sleeves, long coat), red collar & cuffs, gold buttons, silver epaulettes and sash, black Pickelhaube with gold spike and eagle plate, sword on the left. - Soldiers — younger round faces, lighter blue tunics, black belts, rifles with bayonets, Pickelhaube.
- Mayor (bald, white whiskers, black frock coat, gold chain of office), city secretary (thin, round glasses, grey suit, quill), cashier (green visor, black sleeve guards, brown vest), police (black-blue, silver spike), cellmate (cap, stubble, patched coat), judge (black robe and beret), Kaiser (grey-blue uniform, upturned moustache). **Avoid:** real photos or the film's Voigt, invented names/dates, Nazi-era imagery, long sentences on screen.
