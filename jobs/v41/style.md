# v41 – Was passiert, wenn du in den Marianengraben tauchst? · Style bible

**Mood:** cinematic descent, curious and a little claustrophobic; the colour of the water *is* the clock.

**Palette:** sky `#9ED8F0`, surface blue `#3FA9D8` → `#1D6FA3` (200 m) → `#0B2A4A` (1.000 m) → `#06162A` → near-black `#02060E` at the bottom (interpolated from the depth); sphere orange `#FF8A3D` / `#FFB27A` / `#D9662A`; data cyan `#4FD1E8` (depth gauge, arrows), yellow `#FFD166` (light, measurements), bioluminescent green/blue/violet `#7CF5C8` `#6FB8FF` `#C59BFF`, warning red `#FF4D5E`.

**Line & shape:** thin 3–5 px outlines `#0A0E1F`, soft glows, drifting marine snow particles for depth and constant motion.

**Main character:** the viewer inside an orange steel dive sphere (bolted ring, round window with a glowing rim, small headlight on the right that throws a light cone); a simple face with brown hair reacts in the window (calm, wow, cold, happy). Same sphere in every shot. The 1960 bathyscaphe is drawn separately (long float, small sphere, Swiss flag).

**Camera:** side-on ocean cross-section; mostly medium shots of the sphere with the water colour changing, plus diagrams (pressure arrows, colour spectrum, depth column, Everest in the trench, thumb with a small car, moon vs deep-sea scale). Cut every 2–4 s.

**Graphics:** depth gauge top-left counting continuously and hitting `10 M`, `200 M`, `1.000 M`, `3.800 M`, `8.849 M`, `10.900 M` on the spoken words; dark cards with coloured outline for `> 1.000 KG / CM²`, `MOND: 12`, `TIEFSEE: > 27`.

**Subtitles:** `default`. Dust and vignette on.

**Avoid:** monsters, drowning/fear imagery, invented facts.
