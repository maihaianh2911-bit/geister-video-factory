# v40 – Was passiert mit dir, wenn du in ein Schwarzes Loch fällst? · Style bible

**Mood:** cinematic, curious, huge scale — with light comedy in the stretching (rubber/noodle, never body horror).

**Palette:** space indigo gradient `#0B1026` → `#1B2A5A`, star white `#F2F5FF`, sun/disk orange `#FF8A3D` / `#FFC27A` (accretion disk, heat, danger), data cyan `#4FD1E8` (rulers, arrows, badge, cards), warning red `#FF4D5E` (crosses), yellow `#FFD166` (light, photons).

**Line & shape:** thin 3–4 px outlines `#0A0E1F`, soft glows (radial gradients), depth via star field + glowing disk + black hole layers.

**Characters:** astronaut in a white suit with orange stripes, reflective visor (cyan→navy→orange gradient), simple white face shown through the visor when reacting. The friend: dark-haired woman in a small silver droplet-shaped ship with a round window (waves, uses binoculars). Same design in every shot.

**Black holes:** pure black disk, thin bright photon ring, orange accretion disk passing in front and behind, lensed arc over the top. Small one = compact, fast-spinning disk; giant = huge, slow.

**Camera:** wide → zoom to a detail (feet, gauge, galaxy centre, stopwatch); cut every 2–4 s. Distance badge top-left: `20.000 KM` → `1.700 KM` → `HORIZONT`.

**Text:** dark rounded cards with a cyan/orange outline for `10 × SONNE`, `4 MIO. × SONNE`, `≤ 1 MINUTE`, `EINGEFROREN?`; only essential numbers otherwise (`60 KM` ruler, `100 ×` gauge).

**Subtitles:** `default`. Dust and vignette on.

**Avoid:** blood, torn bodies, real scientists' names, invented numbers.
