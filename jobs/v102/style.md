# v102 – Farbe verlieren durch Schlafmangel: style bible

- **Style:** "đầu tròn đen" at the reference2 quality bar (`prompts/styles/dau-tron-den/reference2.svg`), kit copied from v101: black round heads with a soft highlight, white faces, rounded tops with 5 px outline, stick legs, radialGradient ground shadows; medium shot head r≈165, close-up r≈290.
- **Characters:** "du" teal hoodie `#2fa59a` (always in full colour — he does not notice the world fading); colleague: glasses, bun with yellow band, light blue top `#9cc6e6`; researcher: glasses, white coat, clipboard.
- **Key motif:** the world loses colour with every short night. A neutral grey layer with `mix-blend-mode:saturation` (no feColorMatrix) plus a light grey wash is drawn over the set; its strength follows the FARBE meter (top left, rainbow bar with %): 100 → 72 → 3 % in the hook, then Monday 100 → 86, morning 70, Wednesday 50, Friday 26 → 16 %, weekend sleep 42 → 62, Tuesday 100 %. Final shot: colour returns as a ring spreading from du.
- **Sets (3 layers, far layer lightly blurred, one blur group):** warm living room, sunny street with bakery/trees, night bedroom with laptop glow, sunny kitchen, dawn bedroom, office, grey crossroads with traffic light, brick campus, dark blue study graphics (calendar, gauges), night kitchen, Tuesday window with trees.
- **Light & mood:** warm amber at the start, cold blue laptop light at night, pale greys during the week, red warning light for the brain, warm lamp at the weekend, bright sun and saturated green on Tuesday.
- **Text:** day cards MONTAG, MITTWOCH, FREITAG, WOCHENENDE, DIENSTAG; PENNSYLVANIA, 2 WOCHEN, NACH 14 NÄCHTEN, LEISTUNG: WIE NACH EINER DURCHWACHTEN NACHT (red), GEFÜHL: NUR EIN BISSCHEN MÜDE (green), MACH DEN TEST, 1 WOCHE; speech bubbles "Ich bin daran gewöhnt." / "Sechs Stunden reichen mir."; Inter 900, yellow/white with thick black outline, inside y 150–1400.
- **Sound/music:** serious, no comic effects: alarm for the alarm clock, whoosh + thud for the near miss, heartbeat for the brain warning, tings for the 8-hour nights, shimmer when colour returns; composed score documentary → tension → documentary → warm pad.
- **Avoid:** small characters, empty walls, important things at x>920 between y 900–1700, blur filters on glows.
