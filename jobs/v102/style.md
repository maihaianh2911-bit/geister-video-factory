# v102 – Schlaf und Farbe: style bible

- **Style:** "đầu tròn đen" at the reference2 quality bar; kit copied from v101 (black round heads, white faces, 5 px outlines, stick legs, radialGradient light pools and ground shadows, one blurred far-background group per frame).
- **Characters:** "du" teal hoodie `#2fa59a` with light strings (identical in every scene); colleague: glasses, bun with yellow band, light-blue blouse `#a9cbe6`; researchers: white lab coats (glasses / curly hair).
- **Key motif – colour loss:** the world around du loses colour with every short night: a saturation blend overlay (`mix-blend-mode: saturation`, grey rect, opacity = greyness) plus a faint grey wash, drawn over the set but under du, so du stays coloured and does not notice. No feColorMatrix. Greyness: hook demo 0 → .42 → 1; Monday 0 → .2; Wednesday .45; Friday .75–.8; evening .86; weekend falling; Tuesday 0.
- **FARBE meter:** small rainbow bar top-left (y≈130–205) with percentage, always = 1 − greyness; hidden during the study graphics.
- **Sets:** colourful street (hook), night bedroom (blue laptop light, moon), morning window (sky, trees, houses, curtains, sill props), office (window light), crossing with traffic light (pale red), sleep lab (dark violet), amber living room, night kitchen.
- **Study section:** dark violet blurred lab; 14-day calendar with moon + "6 h" in each box; two gauges LEISTUNG (red, falls low) / GEFÜHL (blue, high), captions without numbers.
- **Text:** day cards MONTAG, MITTWOCH, FREITAG, WOCHENENDE, DIENSTAG in yellow Inter 900 with black outline at y≈330; chips for hours; speech bubble "Sechs Stunden reichen mir."
- **Camera:** medium / close-up / wide alternate; every shot pushes in or pans; no still > 4 s.
- **Sound:** serious, no comic effects; alarm for the alarm clock, hit on the late braking, heart under the brain warning, shimmer when colour returns; composed score warm pad → lofi → documentary → (silence) → warm pad major.
- **Avoid:** small characters, important content at x>920 between y 900–1700, text in the subtitle band.
