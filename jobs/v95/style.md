- **Style:** "đầu tròn đen" at the reference2 quality bar (`prompts/styles/dau-tron-den/reference2.svg`): solid black round heads, white faces, rounded tops with 5 px outline, stick legs, soft ground shadows; big characters (medium r≈165, close-up r≈250).
- **Characters:** "du" (woman) teal hoodie `#2fa59a` + small yellow hair clip; roommate: curly hair, mustard sweater `#d9a62e`; colleague: glasses, blue shirt `#4a7fc0`; best friend: bun, purple top, only in the phone video call; researchers: bun + green blouse / glasses + lab coat; students: yellow cap red top / green top.
- **Key object:** a small red loudspeaker `#d9493f` floating next to du's head; sound waves when it talks, static lines when it "knackt"; it turns green `#3c9a5f` (friendly brows) on "Freundlichkeit macht nicht faul" and shrinks at the end.
- **Bubbles:** the speaker's words in red jagged bubbles with white text; du's kind words in white speech bubbles with a pointed tail; exact German text from the script.
- **Sets (3 layers):** bathroom seen from the mirror (tiles, door, frosted window, shelf with bottles, towels, shower curtain, light bar; sharp sink with toothbrush cup), meeting room (blinds, projector chart, whiteboard, plant, chairs; sharp table), canteen (windows with trees, menu board, food counter, other guests; sharp table with tray), living room (books, window, lamp, sofa; sharp coffee table), university library (tall shelves, arched window, ladder, green lamps).
- **Light & mood:** morning bathroom cold grey-blue → next morning warm sun; meeting/canteen neutral daylight; evening call warm amber; split screen warm (friend) vs. cold blue (strict boss).
- **Text:** day cards and statements heavy Inter 900, yellow `#ffd23f` / white / red-orange with thick black outline, inside y 150–1400.
- **Sound/music:** serious, no comic effects; static crackle for the speaker, phone ring, stamp for facts, shimmer for the turn; composed score tension → lofi → warm pad → documentary → warm pad.
- **Avoid:** small characters, empty flat backgrounds, statistics/numbers for the study, anything important at x>920 between y 900–1700.
