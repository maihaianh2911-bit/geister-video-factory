# v94 – Stein im Rucksack: style bible

- **Style:** "đầu tròn đen" at the reference2 quality bar (`prompts/styles/dau-tron-den/reference2.svg`). Solid black round heads (`#1d1d1d`, soft highlight), white faces, rounded hoodies with 5 px outline, stick legs, oval feet, blurred ground shadow.
- **Characters:** "du" = teal hoodie `#2fa59a` with white strings, brown canvas backpack `#8a5a36` (straps in front, bag hanging to the screen-left, grows with each stone). Friends: yellow cap + orange top `#e8742f`; bun with yellow clip + purple top `#8a5bb5`. Zeigarnik: bun, green blouse with collar. Florida psychologists: white lab coats, glasses / curly hair.
- **Key object:** grey stones `#9a9a9c` with carved labels VERMIETER, ZAHNARZT, STEUER, OMA, WÄSCHE (hook: AUFGABE). They fall in on the spoken word (thud), and fall out one by one on Saturday.
- **Framing:** medium shots (head r≈165), close-ups (r≈260–290) for reactions and the thought loop, wide-ish only for the street; never the same framing twice in a row; every shot pushes in or pans.
- **Sets (3 layers):** blurred far background with ≥8 props (bookshelf, window with city, curtain, frames, wall clock, sofa, lamp; street with houses, bakery, tree, bench, bike, bus stop; café with brick, menu board, espresso machine, pendant lamps; bedroom with 03:12 clock, window, poster; kitchen with window, jars, pans, fridge; 1920s study; Florida office) → sharp characters → sharp foreground (table edge with mugs/phone/keys, desk with laptop, teapot).
- **Light & mood:** one light source per set. Mon amber evening lamp, Tue cool blue, Wed grey, Thu warm café turning cold blue in the close-up, Fri night dark blue with red clock glow, good news = warm light flood, Sat/Mon morning bright daylight.
- **Text:** day cards and titles heavy Inter 900, yellow `#ffd23f` or white with thick black outline; thought bubbles white pills with trailing circles; all inside y 150–1400.
- **Sound/music:** serious, no comic effects; real thuds for stones, shimmer for hope, composed score (tension → lofi → documentary → warm pad).
- **Avoid:** small characters, flat empty backgrounds, mirrored text, anything important at x>920 between y 900–1700.
