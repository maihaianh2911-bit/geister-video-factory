# v38 – Wenn du heute deine letzte Zigarette rauchst · Style bible

**Mood:** warm, encouraging, clean health explainer (Kênh 2). Never preachy, never gross: the body is a friendly team that cleans up.

**Palette:** pastel room `#EAF6F4` (base) with stripes `#CFE7E3`, teal `#2EC4B6` (oxygen, good, badges' clock), organ pink `#FF8FA3` / `#E0607A`, navy `#16324F` (all outlines, time badge), warm skin `#F1C6A5`, white cards `#FFFFFF`, smoke grey `#A9B4BE` / `#7D8994` (smoke, CO), alarm red `#E5485F` (risk bars, cross), yellow `#FFD166` (sparkles, warning), blood-vessel reds `#7E2335`–`#F07C92`.

**Line & shape:** flat vector, round shapes, navy outline 5 px; organs and blood cells have simple faces (dot eyes with highlight, curved mouth) that change mood with the story.

**Main character:** man ~40, short brown hair, stubble, grey hoodie with drawstrings and pocket, dark-blue trousers; teal coffee mug. Same design in every shot (balcony, room, stairs, doctor).
Doctors: Ärztin (white coat, teal shirt, dark bun, stethoscope); Arzt (white coat, light-blue shirt, grey hair, glasses).

**Camera:** everyday shot (Altbau balcony, living room, stairwell) → magnifier lens or full-screen "inside the body" (heart, blood vessel, lungs, brain, tongue) → back to the man. New composition every 2–3 s; hard cuts on the timeline marks.

**Graphics:** time badge always top-left (`20 MIN` → `12 STD.` → `TAG 3` → `12 WOCHEN` → `1 JAHR` → `10 JAHRE`), pops on each new mark; white rounded cards for `CO ✗ → O₂ ✓` and `RISIKO ½`; gauges, risk bars and a craving curve instead of text.

**Subtitles:** `default` (dark box). No dust, no vignette.

**Avoid:** gross lungs/tumours, fear imagery, invented numbers or study names, any cigarette glamour.
