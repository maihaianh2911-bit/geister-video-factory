# v97 – Kompliment-Zettel: style bible

- **Style:** "đầu tròn đen" at the reference2 quality bar (`prompts/styles/dau-tron-den/reference2.svg`): solid black round heads, white faces, rounded tops with 5 px outline, stick legs, ground shadows; big characters (medium r≈150–165, close-up r≈215–265).
- **Characters:** "du" teal hoodie `#2fa59a` with strings and pocket (as reference2); colleague: glasses + bun, light blue blouse `#a9cfe8`; new colleague: curly hair (cluster of circles), mustard knit sweater `#d9a62e`; mother: short hair, beige top + red apron `#d2453b` (phone scene: inside a phone frame, her kitchen behind); study participant: yellow cap, green top.
- **Key object:** yellow sticky notes `#ffe066` with handwritten German (Inter 700, ink `#3b3a52`): `Das war richtig gut.` (shoulder), `Danke, du hast meinen Tag gerettet.` (chest), `Bestes Essen!` (belly). An unspoken thought bubble collapses into its note and flies onto du; on Tuesday each note peels off, flies to the praised person and turns into a warm sparkle. Notes glow on "Freude".
- **Sets (3 layers):** evening living room (books, frames, window, sofa, floor lamp; sharp table with mug/phone), meeting room (screen with chart, blinds, whiteboard, chairs, ceiling lights; sharp table), open-plan office (window wall with city, pin board, binders, clock, co-workers; sharp desk with monitor/keyboard/mug), warm dining room (pendant lamp, dresser with plates, family photo, window, clock; sharp table with steaming dish), Chicago skyline by the lake, bright study office, mother's kitchen at night.
- **Light & mood:** hook warm amber → night blue (covered) → neutral daylight (Monday office) → warm amber dinner → cool blue (silence) → bright card graphics for the study → warm glow (Freude) → sunny Tuesday → warm evening call → bright ending.
- **Render cost:** light pools, note glows and floor shadows are radialGradient fills; only the far background uses a blur filter (stdDeviation 3.5–4), once per frame.
- **Text:** day cards (MONTAG, MITTAGS, ABENDS, DIENSTAG) and labels heavy Inter 900 yellow `#ffd23f` / white with thick black outline; study meters without numbers, only `WIE SEHR ES FREUT: UNTERSCHÄTZT` (green arrow up) and `WIE PEINLICH ES IST: ÜBERSCHÄTZT` (red arrow down); all inside y 150–1400.
- **Sound/music:** serious, no comic effects; soft plop for each note landing, shimmer when notes glow/peel, phone buzz for messages/calls, steps on Tuesday; composed score tension → lofi → curious pluck (study) → warm pad.
- **Avoid:** small characters, empty flat backgrounds, statistics/numbers, anything important at x>920 between y 900–1700, blur filters for glows.
