# v80 — So bittest du um Hilfe (style bible)

- **Mood:** calm, serious, practical self-help. Text leads, pictures support; no jokes, no comic sounds.
- **Backgrounds:** flat pastel per section — cream `#fbf1df`, mint `#dcefe6`, lavender `#e7e0f5`, pink `#f8dfe5`, sky `#dce9f7`, butter `#fbefc4`; subtle floor band from y 1345.
- **Ink / text:** `#1f1d2b`, Inter 900, big German key phrases at the top (≈ 76–104 px); keyword marker in yellow `#ffd84a`.
- **Semantic colours:** old/wrong = red `#d94545` (strike-through, ✕, chips "ALT"); new/right = green `#24946a` / `#c4ead7` (✓, chips "NEU"); accents orange `#f08a3c`, purple `#7a5bd0`, blue `#3a6cd6`.
- **Characters:** solid black round-head silhouettes (head, rounded torso, thick limbs, small nose bump shows facing). Fixed identities: *ich* = small hair tuft; *Lisa* = bun + ponytail (designer, purple tag); *Jonas* = white glasses (blue tag); *Felix* = headset (data); *Herr Becker* = white collar + red tie, slightly taller (boss). Name chips under figures on introduction.
- **Poses:** only a few clear presets (stand, bow, deep bow, ask, show, point, think, shrug, cheer, cross arms, sit/type) with 0.4 s blends; no walking, no multi-step handling.
- **Props:** event poster (grey draft vs. colourful layout), phone chat frame, calendar, clock, report/plan documents, meeting-room doors, symbolic book "MENSCHEN FÜR SICH GEWINNEN" (no author/price/QR), cart icon.
- **Method sections:** big coloured title card `n · TITLE`, then ALT bubble (struck out) → NEU bubble (green) → example.
- **Motion:** fade/slide/pop-in on the spoken word; highlights and strikes timed to words. No zoom, shake, particles or flicker.
- **Subtitles:** default dark box; keep all key content within y 150–1400 and away from x > 920 below y 900.
- **Avoid:** whole paragraphs on screen (quotes are shortened), invented facts, comic sfx.
