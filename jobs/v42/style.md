# v42 – Wenn jemand dich unbewusst nachmacht · Style bible

**Mood:** clean, clever, like a nice notebook page (Kênh 5 kinetic typography); light humour from the two figures copying each other.

**Background:** cream paper `#F4EFE6` with a faint square grid `#E3DCCF`, very soft corner shading. Never dark.

**Text cards (top, y ≈ 220–440):** short keyword strips, not one per sentence: white card `#FFFDF8` with ink border and a dashed inner note frame, slight tilt, slides in from the right. Keyword huge, Inter 900, coloured: blue `#2F4BDB` (you), orange `#F28C28` (your counterpart), teal `#14A38B` (good/result), red `#E5484D` (warning, "kein Zufall"), violet `#7A5AF8` (brain); grey `#A8A29A` for the control group. Lead-in line small in serif italic (Liberation Serif) ink `#2B2B2B`. Yellow marker `#FFD84D` swipes under key words; stamps for `KEIN ZUFALL`, `GETESTET`, `NICHT ÜBERTREIBEN`; hand-drawn ✓ / ✗. Owner texts: `KEIN ZUFALL`, `CHAMÄLEON-EFFEKT`, `MEHR TRINKGELD`, `TEST: ZURÜCKLEHNEN`.

**Figures:** simple stick figures, thick ink lines (8–11 px), round white heads with dot eyes: left = you, blue shirt; right = counterpart, orange shirt (grey when the person does *not* mirror). Close-ups use big head-and-shoulders busts of the same two (same colours). Waitress: teal shirt, brown hair bun, white apron, notepad. Researcher: white lab coat, round glasses.

**Sets (change with the content, never the same set-up for more than 2–3 lines):** café wide (warm wall, window, two pendant lamps) · close-up / split-screen mirror portraits · table close-up with glasses · x-ray heads with violet brains · chameleon on a branch (on paper) · lab with tiled wall and one-way mirror · A/B comparison panels · questionnaire close-up · behind-the-mirror POV · Dutch canal street · restaurant with Dutch window · tip saucers close-up · living room with two armchairs + stopwatch. All light, flat, never dark.

**Cards:** a card appears only together with its keyword (never empty); shots that need no keyword have no card.

**Motion:** text appears on the spoken word; the counterpart copies each pose a beat later; cards switch with a quick slide; nothing holds still > 2 s. Thin progress bar with a red dot along the top edge (channel signature).

**Subtitles:** `default`. No dust, no vignette.

**Avoid:** dark backgrounds, long sentences on cards, study author names, manipulation tips.
