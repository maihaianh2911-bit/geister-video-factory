# v101 – Gute Momente im Glas: style bible

- **Style:** "đầu tròn đen" at the reference2 quality bar (`prompts/styles/dau-tron-den/reference2.svg`), kit copied from v99/v98: black round heads with soft highlight, white faces, rounded tops with 5 px outline, stick legs, gradient ground shadows; medium r≈165, close-up r≈290.
- **Characters:** "du" teal hoodie `#2fa59a`; boss: white moustache, grey vest (only in a thought bubble); colleague: bun with yellow band, purple top; sister: two braids with red ties, yellow top (photo on the phone); customer: yellow hair clip, red top; child at the checkout: curly hair, blue top; Martin Seligman: glasses, dark jacket with collar; early human: brown fur tunic.
- **Key motif:** a glass jar (outlined, cork lid, highlight stripe) on du's nightstand. Every good thing becomes a warm golden light (core + radialGradient glow) that flies on a soft arc with a short trail into the jar; the fuller the jar, the bigger the warm glow and the brighter the room.
- **Sets (3 layers, far layer lightly blurred, one blur group):** bedroom (window, wardrobe, posters, shelf with books and plant, chair, jacket) with a sharp bed and nightstand; desk with lamp and diary; sunny street (houses, bakery with awning, trees, lamp posts, bicycle); evening street; café counter; supermarket checkout (shelves with products, KASSE sign, conveyor belt, register); night savanna; lecture hall; study room.
- **Light & mood:** cold dark blue on Monday evening (empty jar), warm lamp light while writing, bright daylight Tuesday, golden sun on the way home, dark violet for the brain graphics (red magnifier → gold), warm amber-orange room on Sunday.
- **Text:** day cards MONTAGABEND, DIENSTAG, SONNTAG, NACH EINER WOCHE, MARTIN SELIGMAN, 7 ABENDE, GLÜCKLICHER, 6 MONATE: Inter 900, yellow/white with thick black outline, inside y 150–1400. Handwritten diary: `1. Kaffee`, `2. Tür aufgehalten`, `3. Foto`, reasons with red WARUM?.
- **Glows/shadows:** radialGradient only, no blur filters on glows.
- **Sound/music:** serious; rising bell tings for every light that lands, shimmer for the glowing jar, switch for the desk lamp, hits only for the danger beat; composed score warm pad → documentary → curious pluck → documentary → lofi → warm pad.
- **Avoid:** small characters, empty walls, comic effects, important things at x>920 between y 900–1700.
