# v96 – Die Sanduhr über deinem Kopf: style bible

- **Style:** "đầu tròn đen" at the reference2 quality bar (`prompts/styles/dau-tron-den/reference2.svg`): solid black round heads with a soft highlight, white faces, rounded tops with 5 px outline, stick legs, gradient ground shadows; big characters (medium r≈150–165, close-up r≈280–290).
- **Characters:** "du" teal hoodie `#2fa59a` (as reference2, no accessory); sister: bun with yellow band, purple knit sweater `#8a5bb5`; colleague (background): glasses, blue shirt `#4a7fc0`; researchers: bun + green blouse / glasses + lab coat; students: yellow cap red top, curly hair blue top, bun mustard top.
- **Key object:** a golden hourglass floating above du's head (gold gradient frame, pale glass, sand `#f2c14e`). Sand runs whenever du uses or thinks about the phone; the lower bulb fills from shot to shot (one shared time function) and is full on "über zwanzig Stunden". Next Monday it is reset almost empty and stays still. Phone screens glow blue via gradients and cast a blue light pool on faces.
- **Sets (3 layers, far layer lightly blurred, one blur group per frame):** evening living room (books, window, lamp, sofa), bedroom at dawn (window with city, curtains, wardrobe, posters, shelf, chair with clothes; sharp nightstand + red digital alarm clock), office (window, chart board, clock, shelves, monitors, printer, coat rack, plant; sharp desk with keyboard/mug), Texas campus (clock tower, trees, lamps), lecture hall (blackboard with puzzles, tiers of students), split exam rooms (window vs. open door to a room with the phone on a shelf), dinner under an orange pendant lamp (dresser with plates, window, frames, plant; sharp table with plates), cold empty room with chairs, sunny kitchen, sunny study, and a two-room cutaway with a door.
- **Light & mood:** amber hook → grey flash-forward → blue-grey dawn → neutral office daylight → bright campus → warm orange dinner turning blue with the two screens → dark blue Sunday → cold void → warm Monday sun.
- **Camera:** alternate medium / close-up / wide; every shot pushes in or pans; nothing stands still longer than ~4 s.
- **Text:** day cards and statements in heavy Inter 900, yellow `#ffd23f` or white with thick black outline, inside y 150–1400; no study numbers (owner).
- **Light pools/glows/shadows:** radialGradient only (no blur filters on glows).
- **Sound/music:** serious; real alarm, phone buzz and door recordings; shimmer for the hourglass, hits for the shocks; composed score tension → lofi → curious pluck (study) → documentary → tension → warm pad.
- **Avoid:** small characters, empty flat walls, comic effects, important things at x>920 between y 900–1700.
