# Lessons (bài học của kênh)

One line per lesson, general enough to apply to any future video. Added after the owner's change requests (newest last).
Max ~40 lines: merge or drop old ones rather than growing forever.

- Props held in a hand stay upright and follow the hand's direction (v13: a bottle was drawn upside down).
- Every character the script names doing something must be drawn doing it on screen (face, hands, prop) — never implied by a closed door; victims show fear (wide eyes, open mouth, raised hands) and infected look infected (grey-green skin, red eyes) from their first frame.
- No picture may hold still for 2 s: change the action or cut to a new angle; two neighbouring shots must not use the same composition.
- Characters get their own design (outfit colours, hairstyle) clearly different from the reference video's characters, kept identical in every shot (older self, close-up arms/hands included); a limb entering a close-up must come from a visible shoulder/body (over-the-shoulder), with a wrist and natural length.
- Subtitles must be readable on every frame: use `"subtitle": "default"` (dark box) whenever any shot has a light or busy background (maps, paper, daylight); `clean` only when the whole video stays dark and the subtitle zone (y 1420–1680) is empty (v31: white text vanished on a cream map and over the basket).
- An animated number reaches the value the voice says by the time that word is spoken, and numbers that belong together (fold count ↔ thickness, year ↔ age) come from one variable so they never disagree on screen (v30: "24× · 13 km"; v31: "980 m²" while the voice said "über zwölfhundert").
- Never put facts on screen that are not in the script or its sources (place names, dates, quantities); when the script stays general, the picture stays general (v31: invented towns where the fabric was bought).
- Frame 0 is the thumbnail: bright, high-contrast hero subject filling the frame, readable in one glance — never a dim or abstract opening (v30 opened on a faint line).
- Never show an empty frame element: a card, board, speech bubble or screen appears only with its content already on it (or filling in during its first 0.3 s); hide it when the line needs none; one set-up (same table, same two characters) must not carry more than 2–3 consecutive lines (v42).
- Frame 0 is the cover: 2–6-word headline + hero subject, big motion before 0.5 s, cut before 1.5 s; never open on a calm establishing shot (v41: viewers left at 0:02, TikTok showed a plain green cover).
- A new picture every 1.5–2.5 s on average (cut, zoom punch, new card, reaction close-up); never one composition longer than 3 s (our videos ran 4–5 s per shot, the well-performing German cat video 1.8 s).
- German audience: serious and clever beats funny — no silly gags, joke sound effects (womp, boing) or absurd comparisons; surprise must come from true facts and clear cause→effect images.

- **Không để video xấu (chủ kênh 30/10).** Trước khi giao, xem lại cả video như người xem trên điện thoại, không chỉ xem từng cảnh. Lỗi phải sửa:
  - chữ bị cắt, tràn hoặc đè lên phụ đề;
  - phụ đề khó đọc;
  - khung trống, hoặc hình đứng yên quá 2 giây;
  - nhân vật méo, mất tay chân, hoặc đổi dáng giữa các cảnh;
  - hình vẽ sơ sài kiểu clip-art rời rạc;
  - màu nhòe, độ tương phản kém;
  - hình không khớp lời đọc hoặc sai số liệu.
  Sau khi dựng, Claude còn kiểm tra lại video hoàn chỉnh. Video lỗi sẽ nhận bình luận `🤖 QA-FIX:` và phải sửa lại.
