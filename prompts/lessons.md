# Lessons (bài học của kênh)

One line per lesson, general enough to apply to any future video. Added after the owner's change requests (newest last).
Max ~40 lines: merge or drop old ones rather than growing forever.

- Props held in a hand stay upright and follow the hand's direction (v13: a bottle was drawn upside down).
- Every character the script names doing something must be drawn doing it on screen (face, hands, prop) — never implied by a closed door; victims show fear (wide eyes, open mouth, raised hands) and infected look infected (grey-green skin, red eyes) from their first frame.
- No picture may hold still for 2 s: change the action or cut to a new angle; two neighbouring shots must not use the same composition.
- Characters get their own design (outfit colours, hairstyle) clearly different from the reference video's characters, kept identical in every shot (older self, close-up arms/hands included); a limb entering a close-up must come from a visible shoulder/body (over-the-shoulder), with a wrist and natural length.
