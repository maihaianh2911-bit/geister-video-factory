# Style "đầu tròn đen" (round black heads)

Chosen by the owner on 02/11/2026 for story videos ("Was wäre, wenn …?" scenarios, dấu hiệu / signs told as scenes). A video uses it when its notes say `PHONG CÁCH: đầu tròn đen`.
Reference frame: `reference.jpg`; its SVG source `reference.svg` already contains the character, bubble, title and background code. Reuse those shapes; do not redraw them in a different way.

## Characters
- Head: a solid black circle (`#1d1d1d`, r ≈ 95–100 at full size) with one soft highlight ellipse (white, opacity .18) at the upper left.
- Face drawn in white only: two round eyes with black pupils (shocked/neutral), arcs for closed/sleepy/happy eyes, a small oval or line for the mouth. No nose, no skin colour.
- Identity comes from one accessory plus the top colour: bun, glasses, cap, hair clip, scarf. Keep each character's accessory and colour identical in every scene.
- Body: a rounded hoodie/sweater/robe shape with a 5 px black outline; hoodie strings as two short light lines.
- Hands: small black dots; arms only when they act (hands on head, pointing, hugging) as thick sleeve-coloured strokes.
- Legs: two black stick legs (10 px) with flat oval feet; a soft ground shadow.

## Scenes
- Background: a recognisable place (classroom, café, living room, street, office) drawn simply and blurred (`feGaussianBlur` ≈ 9) with a light vignette, so the characters stay sharp.
- Thought bubbles: white rounded pills with a black outline and two small trailing circles; dark bold text. A bubble shows the character's thought in German, exactly as spoken.
- On-screen labels (day cards such as `MONTAG`, the hook title): heavy sans-serif, white or yellow `#ffd23f`, thick black outline.
- Keep faces, bubbles and labels inside y 150–1400; subtitles sit lower.

## Motion (never a still image)
- No shot stands longer than about 5 seconds without a change: cut, camera push, a character turning, walking in, or a bubble popping in.
- Always alive: blinking every few seconds, a slight idle bob, bubbles scale in with a small overshoot and fade out.
- Reactions are physical: hands to head, jump back, shrink, lean in.

## Tone
Serious German channel: the situations are everyday and recognisable, humour only from contrast between what someone says and what they think; no slapstick, no comic sound effects.
